Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 5, 2026 App Version: 1.0.0

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Slanotif ("we", "our", or "the App"), represented by package name com.hnkapps.slanotif, handles your information. Slanotif is a utility designed to allow Android users to assign custom notification sounds to specific Slack channels and keywords.

We are deeply committed to user privacy. Slanotif is designed to be entirely private, self-contained, and local to your device.

2. Permissions and Data Access

To provide its core functionality, the App requires access to sensitive device permissions. Specifically:

  • Notification Access (NotificationListenerService): This permission allows Slanotif to read incoming system notifications. Slanotif uses this permission exclusively to identify notifications posted by the official Slack application and inspect their titles and body text to match them against your configured channel rules.

3. Local Processing and Storage

Your privacy is guaranteed by the architecture of Slanotif:

  • No Internet Permission: The App does not request, declare, or use the Android INTERNET permission. It has no technical capability to send your data over the web.
  • No Data Collection or Transmission: Notification text, sender names, channel names, and timestamps are parsed momentarily in memory. None of this data is saved to disk, logged, or transmitted off your device.
  • Local Configuration Storage: The channel names and sound URIs you set in Slanotif are stored locally in your device's secure SharedPreferences cache. This data never leaves your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Slanotif does not integrate with any third-party SDKs, analytics tracking libraries (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), advertising networks, or crash reporting tools that collect or transmit telemetry. The App is 100% dependency-free regarding external trackers.

5. User Control and Data Deletion

You have full control over the permissions granted to Slanotif at any time:

  • You can disable Notification Access for Slanotif at any time through your Android System Settings (Settings > Apps > Special App Access > Notification Access).
  • Clearing the App cache or uninstalling Slanotif completely deletes all your locally stored channel settings and configured preferences.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date above.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via our official GitHub Repository.
