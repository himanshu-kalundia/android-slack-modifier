Per-Channel Sounds

Assign distinct alert sounds to specific channel names, keywords, or sender names. Instantly know if it's a critical production outage or a general chat message.

🤫

Hardware Silence Keys

Stop noisy custom alerts immediately by tapping your power button or volume keys. The app intercepts screen changes to silence alerts without lagging your device.

🛡️

100% Local & Private

Slanotif processes all incoming notifications on your device. We do not use the internet permission, and no information is ever compiled or transmitted to external servers.

Quick Setup Guide

Get up and running with custom Slack sounds in three easy steps.

1. Install the APK

Download the latest release APK from our GitHub repository and install it on your Android device (ensure "Install from Unknown Sources" is enabled).

2. Enable Notification Access

Open Slanotif, tap "Enable Notification Access", and check the permission toggle next to Slanotif. This is required to detect incoming Slack alerts locally.

3. Add Alert Rules

Tap the floating "+" button, type the exact Slack channel or keyword, select a system tone or custom audio file, and tap Save. Add as many rules as you want!
